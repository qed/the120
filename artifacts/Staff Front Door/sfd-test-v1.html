How to flag & report back

✅ works · ⚠️ works-but-odd · ❌ broken. For ⚠️ and ❌ record: what you did · what you expected · what happened · URL · rough time — the notes box on each item captures exactly this, and 📋 Report turns it into a paste-ready summary grouped by severity.

  1. Blockers (❌) — anything that loses a capture, shows names on a dead board, or locks someone out of sign-out
  2. Should-fix (⚠️) — noticeable, especially on the shared iPad
  3. Nice-to-have — polish for later
  4. Confusing but not broken — if it confused you, it'll confuse staff at 9 a.m. on a Saturday

Example: 3.3.5 ❌ — captive portal with 4 queued taps; expected “looks connected but can't reach The 120”; got “try again in a moment” forever. iPad, /fp/fw, ~10:15.

Device findings from 3.3–4 and 3.10–12 are worth more than a green suite — those paths have no other test. Note the Safari version, iPad model, and network shape with each one.

Known and expected — do not flag these
  • The hub's FW card saying “No upcoming weekends” — true: all four rehearsal cohorts were archived by migration (Unit 10). The honest zero is the launch state.
  • “Archived by: unrecorded” on the four rehearsal cohorts — a migration archived them, and a migration is not a human.
  • The bar showing the account email rather than a name — no display name exists.
  • An archived cohort still accepting guide check-in and quick-create — deliberate and tested; archiving is staff visibility plus a closed board, not a disabled cohort.
  • Unarchiving not restoring the board token — tokens are one-way; mint a new one.
  • A non-staff account signing in at /crm/login landing on a 404 at /staff — recorded, functionally identical to the old dead end.
  • A sign-out refused with clear_failed (a cache clear threw) — an open decision awaiting device evidence; record how often it fires and what the person did next.
  • Internal names still containing “path” (path_cohorts, path-sw-* caches, fw.cacheOwner) — deliberately unchanged.
