# Parent notice — additions to what First Profit stores (to send: owner, from personal email)

**To:** goldlist@gmail.com, kkliman@gmail.com, lee@camendola.com
**Subject:** Two small additions to what First Profit stores

Hi — a quick heads-up about two additions to First Profit, both built to make the game better for your kid:

1. **Birth year.** The game may ask your child their birth year once. We use it only to pick age-appropriate wording for their tasks — nothing else.
2. **"Stuck? Tell us" notes.** Every task now has a small button where your child can tell us they're stuck, with an optional short note. We use these notes only to improve First Profit, we remind kids not to include names or addresses, and we delete them after twelve months.

One more thing worth knowing: your child's storefront page lives at firstprofit.school followed by their first name (for example, firstprofit.school/cedric). If you'd rather use a different name there, just tell us and we'll change it.

Both additions are covered in our updated consent notice (version 2026-08-03.1). If you'd rather your child not use either, just reply and we'll sort it out. Thanks for testing with us!
