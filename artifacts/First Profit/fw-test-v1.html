How to flag & report back

✅ works · ⚠️ works-but-odd · ❌ broken. For ⚠️ and ❌ record: what you did · what you expected · what happened · URL · rough time — the notes box on each item captures exactly this, and 📋 Report turns it into a paste-ready summary grouped by severity.

  1. Blockers (❌) — must fix before September planning resumes
  2. Should-fix (⚠️) — noticeable, especially anything a guide hits mid-loop
  3. Nice-to-have — polish for later
  4. Confusing but not broken — if it confused you, it'll confuse a guide at 9 a.m. on a Saturday

Example: 3.10.3 ❌ — tapped Not-yet on a checked row; expected one-tap flip; got a refusal telling me to undo first. iPad, /fp/fw, ~10:15.

Deliberately changed — do not flag these
  • The First Dollar confirm dialog is gone on purpose (decision 2026-07-28). First dollar checks instantly like every other step; a stray tap ringing the bell is the accepted risk. Don't flag the missing "this rings the bell" dialog.
  • Batch check-in ("Same tap for someone else?") is removed as a capability, including grouped bell celebrations — a three-kid team's first dollars now celebrate one at a time.
  • "— Recorded" lines and the "Next student" button are gone; the icon state and the left nav replaced them.
  • The per-task page is retired — old task URLs redirect to the student page (never 404). That redirect is correct behavior, not a bug.
  • The attestation checkbox is gone — the notice is covered by online registration; quick-create silently stamps provenance. Don't flag the missing checkbox.
  • The Switch link is gone — the weekend name in the header is the way back to the picker.
  • Archived weekends still accept check-ins — queued offline work must be able to drain after archiving. By design.
  • Internal names still containing "path" (path-sw-* caches, path-offline-queue) — deliberately unchanged.
